Automap redesign: reference mockup

Drawn from what the real map code shows for two real saves: the draw lists at every vision and spoiler level, the tooltips as the game builds them, the press chains, the fog from the saves' .map files, and the game's own pictures. Only the drawing is the mockup. Design: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-01-automap-look-and-controls-design.md (local).
Mouse: left-drag pans, the wheel zooms (no further out than the whole level), middle-click centres on the party, right-click opens your notes (and the debug rows), Shift shows the whole press chain at ALL, Shift+wheel scrolls a long tooltip, Ctrl+wheel sizes it, the side buttons or Page Up/Down change level. Keys: / or Ctrl+F searches, Escape closes.
Places: before the win, L11 has press chains with goal badges, L4 teleporters, L2 and L7 spinners; mid game shows fog and levels not visited yet. Add ?save=1&L=4&v=2&s=0&cs=48&hh=x,y to the address for a fixed view.
