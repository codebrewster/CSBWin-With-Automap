Dungeon Master Calculator

What heroes, monsters and spells do to each other in Dungeon Master and Chaos Strikes Back, worked out from the game engine's own formulas.

Loading the calculator (about 5 MB, kept by the browser after the first time)…

  • Everything runs in your browser. A savegame you open stays on your computer.
  • Savegames of the Atari ST, the Amiga (also .adf disks), CSBwin and CSBwin Plus.
  • The built-in tables of both games are used; choose the one of your game under Tables in the side panel.
  • Zoom with your browser: Ctrl + and Ctrl −.
